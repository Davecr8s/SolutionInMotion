SIM COMPETENCIES

Solution In Motion Group is made up of Solution in Motion Inc registered in Maryland, the USA with the objective of managing the software and technology distributing channels and Solution in Motion Limited, a privately owned specialized Technology consulting company focused on end-to-end solutions for the financial sector.

Solution In Motion Limited has become one of the leading technology distributors, and resellers providers across sub-Saharan Africa, with satisfied clients from the following sectors – Financial Service Institutions (FSI), Insurance, Public sector Institutions, and the Oil and Gas sector.

Asides from our core services, we also offer consultancy, training and technical support to our numerous clients.

Some of the main areas of focus includes:

Software Security Solutions

We are committed to delivering cutting-edge technology solutions majoring in these verticals; Enterprise Application, Security, Advanced Threat Protection, Digital Transformation.

Software Reseller

Providing innovative software solutions for enterprise development and business solutions across sectors.

Software Distributor

We are a Value-Added distributor of Best-of-Breed, Best-In-Class software solutions used by Professionals, Service Providers and Value-Added Resellers across Africa.

Aircraft Maintenance and Inventory Management:

Support the aeronautical world with the best-of breed maintenance software solutions, Inventory management and tools aimed at reducing downtime and improving aircraft maintenance operations.

3D Seaport Services:

Services (through our partners conduct intermodal and logistics, terminal operating systems, port management and operations evaluations, container terminal planning.

Solar Technology:

Our World class solar solutions are designed to innovatively answer the power, efficiency and Sustainability questions.

We offer you the capability to reinvent and remodel your operations and business with current methodologies.

SIM prides itself on delivering products and services which adhere to the principles of quality, transparency and affordability.
