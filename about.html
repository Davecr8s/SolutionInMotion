About Solution In Motion Limited

Solution In Motion (SIM) Limited is a subsidiary of (Solution In Motion Inc, USA) SIM Limited is a specialized IT company that focuses on the provision of products and services to financial institutions, Insurance, PFAs, Telcos, and Public sector institutions among others. Since its inception in 2014, Solution In Motion (SIM) Limited prides itself on delivering products and services which adhere to the principles of quality, professionalism, and affordability.

Solution In Motion Limited's operational office location here in Lekki Peninsula (Plot 10, Justice Chime Blvd Off Olokonla Rd, KM 47 Lekki Express Way) – Victoria Island extension, Lagos – is a very strategic one as we are in one of the most engaging tech communities in the whole Nigeria, which therefore means that we are close to all the relevant tech companies, FSI, Insurance, PFAs, Oil & gas and other assorted stakeholders. In view of this, we are prepared to go the extra mile in ensuring that we build a solid business structure. We are prepared to source for and recruit only the best employees that will help grow and sustain our cyber security business.
